  n=0 with modrm_out STILL SET. The naive hexa port returned an all-zero

  on the two short-buffer failure paths to match C's output-param contract.
  This is the value of bit-exact RUNEQ: it surfaced a write-before-fail
  side-effect that a spec-only port would have silently dropped.
